# Evidence checks for case reviewers

This is evaluation support, not router runtime guidance. Keep it and case
expectations outside the answering model's context during independent tests.

Use checks relevant to the claim; do not add unrelated work to a narrow question.
A citation identifier establishes identity, not entailment. For every decisive
claim record the source/version, location, what it actually supports, and any
access limitation. Retrieval receipts and worker summaries are not substitutes
for reading the passages on which the answer depends.

- Separate author statements, observations, model assumptions, your inference,
  and unresolved uncertainty. Preserve qualifiers such as “suggests” when
  evaluating an author's claim. Lack of a located test is not proof the authors
  never ran it; specify which sections/SI were actually inspected.
- Before alleging a numerical inconsistency, check the original figure, caption,
  table and relevant source-data headers together. Record any unresolved mapping
  conflict rather than selecting the convenient label. Verify units, normalization,
  conditions and direction of comparison; micro- and milli- are not interchangeable.
- Distinguish cumulative levels from adjacent step differences; derive a claimed
  limiting step from the defined sequence, not the highest plotted ordinate.
  State reference states and computational conditions before comparing energies.
- Dataset-average error does not give an individual prediction interval or prove
  the ordering of near-tied candidates. Do not manufacture uncertainty bounds.
- Compare performance only under sufficiently comparable temperature, loading,
  electrolyte, normalization and other relevant conditions. If they differ, qualify
  the comparison instead of ranking intrinsic superiority.
- Verify bibliographic identity, publication/version date, and the specific support
  separately. Do not infer dates from DOI strings or volume years. A real paper may
  contradict rather than support the attached claim. Mark unread/paywalled claims
  unverified; do not fill gaps from memory or a secondary summary.
- For novelty, map the exact claimed contribution to examined prior work. Bound
  the conclusion by search and reading coverage; later work cannot establish
  pre-cutoff priority. Do not invent author motivations, percentages of novelty,
  or omissions based only on an abstract or peer-review file.

These checks guide source reading. They are not an automated scientific oracle,
and passing them is not evidence that this skill improves accuracy on new papers.
